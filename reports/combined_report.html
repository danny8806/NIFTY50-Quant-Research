The analysis includes benchmark comparison, risk-adjusted performance metrics, and actionable recommendations.

7. Conclusions

1. Best Overall Strategy: MACD delivers the most consistent risk-adjusted returns across stocks with the highest average Sharpe ratio. It outperforms on RELIANCE (794.57% return) and INFY (733.03% return).


2. Stock-Specific Suitability: RSI strategy excels on large-cap liquid stocks (TCS: 1014.87% return) but fails on growth stocks (INFY: 18.23% return). Strategy selection must be stock-specific.


3. Drawdown Risk: All strategies experienced significant drawdowns (50-80%) during market crises (2008, 2020), highlighting the need for robust risk management.


4. Buy & Hold vs Active: Over the full 26-year bull market period, Buy & Hold generally outperformed active strategies, demonstrating the challenge of market timing in long-term bull runs.


5. Win Rates: RSI and Bollinger (mean-reversion strategies) show higher win rates (63-83%) vs trend-following strategies (26-39%), but trend strategies capture larger individual wins.

8. Recommendations

1. Use MACD as a baseline for multi-stock portfolios - best risk-adjusted returns across different stock types.


2. Apply RSI selectively - excellent for mega-cap liquid stocks, avoid on volatile growth names.


3. Optimize parameters - default settings (20/50 EMA, 14 RSI) may not be optimal; grid search can improve results.


4. Build an ensemble model - combine MACD for trend direction with RSI for entry timing.


5. Add risk management - trailing stops, position sizing, and portfolio-level limits to reduce drawdown severity.


6. Extend to all 15 stocks - include sector-wise decomposition for better diversification insights.


7. Incorporate regime detection (ML models) to dynamically select the best strategy for current market conditions.
